About

Sofiya Baishulakova is a journalism student at the Medill School of Journalism, Northwestern University in Qatar, majoring in Journalism, Media and Integrated Marketing Communications with minors in AI & Media and Strategic Communication.

Her work began in a national newsroom in Kazakhstan, cutting raw footage into broadcast segments for the Khabar television channel. Since then she has produced healthcare content for a pediatric rehabilitation clinic, covered events in Almaty, and reported and filmed across campus and student media in Doha. She works in Adobe Creative Cloud and DaVinci Resolve, and moves between reporting, filming, and editing on the same story.

She holds a 3.8 GPA and has been named to the Dean's List in both her first and second years. She is the founder and lead organizer of EduHACK, a student initiative that has run more than 15 campaigns and 25 workshops for over 100 students.
